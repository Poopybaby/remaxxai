Hollow Creek trails

Seven marked trails through old cedar forest, two waterfalls, and one very patient heron.

Cedar Loop

Flat boardwalk through the oldest trees. Stroller friendly.

2.1 kmEasy · 40 min

Twin Falls

Follows the creek up to both waterfalls. Slippery after rain.

6.4 kmModerate · 2 h

Ridge Line

Steep switchbacks to the lookout. Bring water, skip it in fog.

11 kmHard · 4.5 h

Heron Pond

A short walk to the pond. Quiet mornings are best.

1.4 kmEasy · 25 min
Trailhead parking opens at sunrise. Dogs welcome on leash.
